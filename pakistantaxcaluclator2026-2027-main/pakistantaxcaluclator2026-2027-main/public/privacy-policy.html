Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Money Tools provides financial calculators and general educational information. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use this website.

Calculator information

Amounts and other values you enter into the tax, salary and expense calculators are processed in your browser. Money Tools does not operate an account system and does not ask you to submit those calculator entries to us. Avoid entering passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information into a calculator.

Analytics

This website may use Ahrefs Web Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the service. Analytics providers may process technical information such as page views, device or browser information and approximate usage data under their own privacy terms.

Advertising and cookies

Money Tools uses Google AdSense advertising code. If ads are enabled or served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your device, measure advertising and, where permitted by your settings and applicable law, personalize ads. You can manage advertising and privacy choices through Google's available controls and your browser settings.

Third-party data services

The currency converter requests reference exchange-rate information from ExchangeRate-API. The gold-rate tool requests spot-price information from Gold-API. Those services receive the technical information needed to answer the requests and are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Their displayed rates are indicative only.

Links to other websites

This website may link to external sources. We do not control the content, security or privacy practices of third-party websites.

Data security and retention

We do not maintain user accounts or a Money Tools database of calculator entries. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies. No method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its services change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions or website feedback, use the Money Tools project support page on GitHub.
